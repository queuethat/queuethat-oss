# Accessibility statement (draft)

**Status: draft. Not published until the audit in `kiosk-audit.md` is signed off.**

We want everyone to be able to join a queue. The self check-in kiosk aims to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA
and the terminal clauses of EN 301 549.

## What the kiosk offers
- An **Accessibility** button on every screen: high contrast, text at twice the size.
- Operation with a keyboard or any switch device that sends keys.
- **Audio guidance** (in accessibility mode): the screen, each control you move to, and your ticket
  are spoken. Use headphones where the venue provides a jack.
- A warning 20 seconds before an idle reset, with **I need more time** (adds 2 minutes).
- Your accessibility settings reset when you finish, so the next guest starts fresh.

## Known limits
- No tactile keypad.
- Spoken guidance uses the voices installed on the device; some languages may sound basic or be
  unavailable.
- The audit on real devices is not complete.
